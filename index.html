04Our Story

Italian Obsession. UAE Home.

In 1992, Horacio Pagani set out to prove that a car could be a work of art without sacrificing an ounce of performance. Every chassis is still hand-laid in carbon-titanium inside the Modena atelier, every detail machined, stitched, and finished by a single craftsperson.

Pagani Dubai brings that obsession to the UAE — a private showroom built for collectors who understand that true luxury is measured in millimetres, not marketing.

“Amongst the clouds you will find the perfect union of art and science.” — Horacio Pagani
